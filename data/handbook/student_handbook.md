# Northfield Institute of Management — Student Handbook (Fictional, 2026-27)

This handbook is a fictional document created for the CampusPilot AI demo. It does not describe a real institution.

## 1. Fee Payment and Due Dates

1.1 Programme fees are paid in three instalments: 40% at admission, 30% by 15 September, and 30% by 15 November of each academic year.

1.2 Fees can be paid through the online payment portal, by demand draft payable to "Northfield Institute", or at the Accounts Office counter during working hours (Monday to Friday, 10:00 to 16:00).

1.3 A late fee of 2% of the overdue instalment per month, or part of a month, applies after a grace period of 7 calendar days from the due date.

1.4 Students with overdue fees beyond 30 days may be barred from registering for the next term and from collecting hall tickets until the dues are cleared.

## 2. Payment Portal Failures

2.1 If the payment portal fails or the transaction is declined, the student must save a screenshot of the error and any transaction reference number.

2.2 The student must raise a support request with the Accounts Office within 24 hours of the failed payment, attaching the screenshot or transaction reference.

2.3 If the failure is documented and reported within 24 hours and before the due date, a payment made on the next working day will not attract a late fee.

2.4 If the failure is not reported within 24 hours, the normal late fee under section 1.3 applies.

2.5 Amounts debited from the student's bank account but not credited by the portal are reconciled within 7 working days, and the student does not need to pay twice.

## 3. Refunds and Withdrawal

3.1 A student who withdraws within 15 days of the start of the programme receives a refund of fees paid, less an administrative charge of Rs. 10,000.

3.2 No tuition fee refund is made after 15 days from the start of the programme. The hostel caution deposit is refundable as per section 8.5.

3.3 Approved refunds are processed to the original payment source within 30 working days.

## 4. Scholarships

4.1 The Institute offers merit scholarships, need-based grants, and diversity scholarships. Awards are credited as an adjustment against tuition fees and are not paid in cash.

4.2 A merit scholarship is renewed each year if the student maintains a CGPA of at least 3.0 and an attendance of at least 75%.

4.3 Scholarship applications are reviewed within 30 working days of the submission deadline. Students can check the status of their application with the Scholarship Office.

4.4 A student on a scholarship whose CGPA falls below 3.0 is placed on probation for one term. The scholarship is withdrawn if the CGPA is not restored to 3.0 in that term.

4.5 Need-based grants require income documents, which must be submitted again every year.

## 5. Examinations

5.1 Students must carry their Institute ID card to every examination. Entry is not permitted more than 30 minutes after the exam has started.

5.2 Mobile phones, smart watches, and unauthorised notes are not allowed in the examination hall.

5.3 A student may apply for re-evaluation of an answer script within 7 calendar days of the result announcement, on payment of Rs. 500 per paper.

5.4 A make-up examination may be granted to a student who misses an exam for a documented emergency. The application must be submitted within 3 working days of the missed exam, with supporting documents.

5.5 A student must have the minimum attendance required under section 6 to be eligible to sit an end-term examination.

## 6. Attendance

6.1 A minimum attendance of 75% in each course is required to appear in the end-term examination.

6.2 Students with attendance between 65% and 75% may apply for condonation to the Programme Office, with valid documents. Condonation is granted at most once per academic year.

6.3 Attendance below 65% cannot be condoned and the student must repeat the course.

6.4 Representing the Institute in an approved event is treated as present attendance when the faculty coordinator confirms it in writing.

## 7. Library

7.1 Students may borrow up to 4 books at a time for 14 days. The loan can be renewed once for another 7 days, if no other student has reserved the book.

7.2 The overdue fine is Rs. 5 per book per day. Borrowing privileges are suspended while fines are unpaid.

7.3 Reference books, journals, and past examination papers cannot be taken out of the library.

7.4 A lost or damaged book must be replaced, or the student must pay its current price plus a processing fee of Rs. 200.

7.5 The library is open from 08:00 to 22:00 on weekdays and from 09:00 to 17:00 on Saturdays. It is closed on Sundays.

## 8. Hostel

8.1 Hostel rooms are allotted on a first-come basis after the admission fee is paid. Room changes can be requested only at the start of a term.

8.2 The hostel gate closes at 22:30. Students returning later must have prior written permission from the warden.

8.3 Visitors are allowed in the common area only, between 10:00 and 18:00. Visitors may not enter rooms.

8.4 Cooking appliances, heaters, and other high-power electrical appliances are not allowed in rooms.

8.5 The hostel caution deposit of Rs. 15,000 is refunded within 30 working days after the student vacates the room, after deducting any damage charges.

## 9. Grievance Redressal

9.1 A student can submit a grievance to the Grievance Cell through the grievance form, email, or a support request.

9.2 The Grievance Cell acknowledges every grievance within 2 working days and aims to resolve it within 15 working days.

9.3 If the student is not satisfied with the resolution, an appeal can be filed with the Dean of Students within 7 working days of the decision.

9.4 Grievances about harassment are handled confidentially by the Internal Committee, and the complainant is protected against retaliation.

## 10. Academic Calendar and Deadlines

10.1 Course registration for each term closes on the 5th working day of the term.

10.2 Students can add or drop a course, or change an elective, within the first 10 working days of the term. The request is made through the Programme Office and needs the approval of the programme coordinator.

10.3 A course dropped after the 10th working day appears on the transcript with a "W" grade and the fee is not refunded.

10.4 Internal assessment marks are published within 10 working days of each assessment. Queries about marks must be raised within 5 working days of publication.

10.5 A student who needs a bonafide or fee certificate should apply at the Programme Office. It is issued within 3 working days.

## 11. Academic Integrity

11.1 Plagiarism, copying, and impersonation in any assessment are serious offences.

11.2 A first offence leads to zero marks in the assessment. A repeat offence can lead to suspension for one term.

11.3 The use of AI tools in an assessment is permitted only when the course instructor has allowed it in writing, and the use must be declared in the submission.

## 12. ID Cards

12.1 Every student receives an Institute ID card, which must be carried on campus at all times.

12.2 A lost ID card must be reported to the Security Office. A replacement card is issued within 3 working days on payment of Rs. 300.
